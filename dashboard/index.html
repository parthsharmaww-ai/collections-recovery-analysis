Collections recovery · Is the "+11% month-on-month" real?

Data to 2026-08-08 · analysis window Jan–Jul 2026 · 30,000 accounts · rebuilt from 17 raw tables
What happened

The +11% isn't real. Recovery has been flat all year.

The only +11% month is Feb→Mar (+11.0%): March simply has 31 days vs February's 28. Per day, Feb→Mar was +0.3% (95% CI -6% to +7%).

Jan–Jul trend: -0.35% per month, not significant (CI -1.1% to +0.3%). No segment, cohort, channel or campaign improved.

Decision needed · ₹10 Cr

Approve ₹0.75 Cr for a WhatsApp/digital test now. Hold ₹9.25 Cr for a month-6 gate.

Release the rest only if a randomised 50/50 test shows at least +0.20 pp lift in monthly payment rate. Confidence: low. No option is proven by today's data.

Fix before trusting
these numbers

Agent IDs are shared: 1,000 IDs, 643 codes, 10 names; 194 IDs logged in twice at once. Agent productivity can't be measured.
